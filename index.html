What is included?

The four COTC studies are COTC021, COTC022, COTC026 and COTC030. This browser joins Case_Summary_303.csv and Case_Expression_303.csv by Case ID; it does not store or serve raw sequence reads.

RNA-seq / NanoString flags describe presence in the specific audited ICDC manifest; GEO sample IDs and published PBMC evidence are separate fields.

Important interpretation notes

OS = overall survival; DFS/DFI = disease-free survival / interval as defined by each source. Time values and original censor codes were retained from the published supplements; event definitions have not been harmonized.

Sample-name discrepancies and missing follow-up are explicitly flagged. This is a discovery and data-integration resource, not a clinical prediction tool.
